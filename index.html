DIGITAL SAFETY GUIDE

What to do — and what not to do

Sentinel AI turns its assessment into practical safety guidance you can act on.

✓

DO

  • Save evidence such as screenshots when appropriate.
  • Block or report harmful accounts.
  • Tell a trusted adult or trusted person.
  • Use strong, unique passwords and privacy settings.
  • Ask for help when an interaction feels unsafe.
×

DON'T

  • Don't share passwords, OTPs, private photos, or sensitive details.
  • Don't meet an unknown online contact secretly.
  • Don't click suspicious links or claim unexpected prizes.
  • Don't continue an escalating argument with a threatening person.
  • Don't feel pressured to keep an unsafe interaction secret.
